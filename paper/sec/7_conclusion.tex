\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We have shown that motion subspaces provide a behavior-level representation for comparing design-intent-aware sketch constraints. They absorb command-level diversity among human annotations while exposing differences in driven motion $\theta_\mathrm{motion}^\mathrm{full}$, relation-preserving motion $\theta_\mathrm{motion}^\mathrm{rel}$, dimensional placement $S_\mathrm{place}$, and relation-violating edits $\theta_\mathrm{leak}^\mathrm{dim}$. These metrics separate held-out humans from model predictions, respond predictably to controlled constraint removal, and provide RL signals that reduce dimensional shortcuts. Dimensional placement remains the key open direction, because our pseudo-reference training data cannot supervise it; improving this signal would contribute to more design-intent-aware CAD generation.
